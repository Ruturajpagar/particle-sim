What is and isn't in here

In: Coulomb, the uncertainty principle (as a pair rule), spin and Pauli exclusion, a heat bath. Constants: ħ, masses, charges, spins, in atomic units.

Not in: strong and weak forces (nuclei are given), relativity, photons (the bath stands in), gravity.

This live engine uses the fast quasi-classical rules (Phase 2) so hundreds of particles run in real time. They form atoms and molecules from free particles but overbind molecules. The exact quantum engine (Phases 4–5 in the repository) reproduces H through Be within 0.5% but takes an hour per atom.
